Book it. Use it. Hand it over on time.

A booking app for shared rooms and equipment on campus. Every booking is tied to your ID, has a fixed length and a clear end time, and the app helps you start and finish on time.

Scan to try it on your phone.

What changed from v1

  • From one 3D printer to any shared space or machine
  • Booking by ID, with a fixed length from 15 min up
  • See how many people are in a room before you go
  • Check in, report damage, countdown and check out
  • Booking list at the top, limits always shown
